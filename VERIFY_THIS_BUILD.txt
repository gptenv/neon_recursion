Neon Recursion WebGL/WASM V10.7

Expected app version marker:
  webgl-wasm-v10.7-2026-06-10

Fixed/added:
- V10.7 layered reactive shader engine with underlay, warped camera layer, previous-frame displacement, overlay masks, and pseudo-alpha compositing.
- V10.7 shader tuning restores saturated recursive contrast, brighter colour cycling, stronger feedback displacement, and visible audio/video motion while keeping camera structure readable.
- Up/Down and vertical swipe-hold now control visible visual drive for warp, colour, depth, feedback, and reaction gain.
- Generated presets use a new 24-value schema for much broader audio/video/reactive variance.
- The 200 baked presets were regenerated and remapped through a mixed family/mode recipe permutation for stronger adjacent-slot variance.
- Recipes select distinct audio reaction lanes, motion lanes, shape transforms, mask reactions, and feedback personalities for stronger preset-to-preset behavioral variance.
- -/= decrement/increment a live randomizer seed offset that reseeds the active recipe and future forged presets.
- New forged presets use browser entropy plus multiple candidate recipes biased toward recipe-lane distance from existing presets.
- F toggles browser fullscreen via the Fullscreen API.
- Pressing F always forces the HUD hidden; it never toggles HUD state.
- H remains an independent HUD toggle and can show the HUD while fullscreen is active.
- Fullscreen changes trigger a resize pass.
- Single click/tap forges the same generated preset that C forges, while swipes do not.
- Double-click, double-tap, and Space force fullscreen, hide the HUD, and toggle MP4/H.264 canvas recording/download.
- Recording object URLs are revoked after download initiation and on page unload.
- Recording copies each WebGL frame into a stable offscreen recording canvas and requests capture frames from that canvas to avoid Chrome/macOS fullscreen-canvas first-frame-only output.
- Recording start waits for fresh fullscreen-sized app frames after entering fullscreen before MediaRecorder is created.
- Mic audio and browser-shared system/tab audio are mixed for both audio reactivity and MP4 recording audio.
- Axis defaults remain: X=ON, Y=ON, Z=OFF.
- The bundled touchstone.wasm make hotfix remains.
- The WebGL sat3() shader hotfix remains.
